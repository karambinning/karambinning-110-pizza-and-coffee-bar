What 110 is

A neighbourhood evening kitchen — small menu, hot oven, no fuss.

Pizza

Thin, crispy, stone-baked bases. Classic margheritas through to fully-loaded specials. Built to order, out in minutes.

Chicken & halal

Grilled chicken plates, halal-friendly toppings, sandwiches, sides. Honest food for a hungry walk home from town.

Coffee & bites

Coffee bar by name, coffee bar in practice — proper brews alongside garlic bread, sides and a small dessert list.
