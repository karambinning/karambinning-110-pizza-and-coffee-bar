About

Two things, done well.

110 Pizza and Coffee Bar is a small, independent spot in Loughborough. We do two things: pizza and coffee. And we try to do both of them properly.

The idea is simple. A coffee bar where the espresso is pulled fresh and the milk is steamed right. A pizza counter where the dough is rested, the base is thin, and the toppings aren't piled on for show.

We're built for the everyday — the morning flat white before work, the long lunch with a friend, the takeaway pizza on a Friday night. No fuss, no theatre, just a warm room and food we'd want to eat ourselves.

A neighbourhood place. Casual. Friendly. Open to anyone who fancies a slice and a coffee — together or one without the other.
