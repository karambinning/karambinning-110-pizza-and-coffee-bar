About

A family kitchen on Clarence Street.

110 has been feeding hungry locals, late workers and students for years now — a small, family-run pizza and coffee bar where the oven stays hot, the menu stays honest, and the door stays open every evening of the week.

You'll find us at number sixty-five Clarence Street, a few minutes' walk from Loughborough's market square. The kitchen runs on a simple promise — wood-fired pizza, made to order, served fresh, no shortcuts.

"Best pizza in Loughborough" — and we don't mind the customers saying so.

The "coffee bar" half of the name is more than decoration. Alongside the pizza there's proper coffee, hot drinks, garlic bread, grilled chicken, halal-friendly options, and a small list of sides and desserts. Whatever you fancy after the pubs shut, before the gig, or on the way home from work, the lights are on.

We're family-run, which means the same people who answer the phone are usually the ones rolling the dough. It's not a chain, it's not a franchise, and we like it that way.
